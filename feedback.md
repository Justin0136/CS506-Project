Very thorough proposal. The topic, timeline, goals, and data collection plan are all clear, and the goals are specific and measurable with explicit metrics and baselines. My main concern is the reporting bias you already flagged. Level 2 crashes are only reportable if they involve a vulnerable road user, a fatality, an airbag deployment, or a hospital transport. That means for Level 2, the injury label is close to being determined by the reporting criteria themselves. Your "crash partner" feature (pedestrian/cyclist) is also one of those criteria. As a result, the model may mostly learn "Level 2 or hit a pedestrian -> injury," which would look good on PR-AUC without telling you much. I'd strongly consider making the ADS-only injury model the primary analysis, or at least reporting results separately by automation type from the start rather than as an afterthought. The monthly weather context from Open-Meteo is a reasonable idea, but it's a weak signal given that the report already records crash-time weather. If time gets tight, that's a good thing to cut.
